##In-Class Activities
###Data Exploration
Data Source 1: Auckland Transport Information
Source:
Auckland Transport public transport information.
Data Includes:
• Different bus routes
• Different bus stops
• Timetables
*
Structure:
The data is organised by route, stop location, and estimated travel time.
Limitations:
All information available through Auckland Transport is planned transport data. However, in everyday life there are many unexpected situations such as bus cancellations, delays, and traffic incidents. These datasets do not fully reflect real commuting experiences.
Data Source 2: Self-recorded Commuting Data
Source:
Personal commuting observations and records.
Data Includes:
• Travel route
• Transport mode
• Commuting time
Structure:
The data is collected by recording daily commuting activities.
Limitations:
The dataset is currently small and only represents a limited range of commuting experiences.

Independent Study
Consultation Reflection
The Proposal Consultation helped me understand my project direction more clearly.
I received feedback that my topic about public transport and everyday experience was clear, but I needed to explain more clearly why the project matters and why I want to explore this topic.
This feedback encouraged me to think beyond the data itself. For example, public transport is not only a way of travelling, but also something that influences people's daily routines and commuting experiences.
After this discussion, I became more interested in exploring how commuting experiences can be represented through data visualisation. At this stage, I am mainly focusing on routes and commuting times as a starting point for further exploration.
Technical Skill Building
For this activity, I explored hand-drawn data visualisation.
I looked at several illustrated maps and hand-drawn information graphics to understand how drawing can communicate information in a more personal and engaging way.
I then created three rough concept sketches.
Through sketching, I learnt that data visualisations should begin with simple information before gradually becoming more complex. If too much information is included at the beginning, the visualisation can become confusing.
This helped me understand the importance of developing the project gradually. At the moment, I am focusing on routes and commuting times before adding more layers of information in future weeks.
