# Week 08
## In-Class Activities
### Progress Reports

This week I presented my selected concept direction, Comparing Commuting Experiences.
The project aims to compare commuting routes and travel times between different university students through a hand-drawn visualisation.
During discussion, several useful comments were provided.
One suggestion was to make the comparison between students more visually obvious so that viewers could quickly identify differences between journeys.
Another suggestion was to keep the visual system simple and avoid including too many layers of information at the same time.
A further comment suggested using the University of Auckland as a common starting point, as this would make route comparisons easier to understand.
These comments helped me think more carefully about how information should be organised within the visualisation.
Critical Design Propositions
Through discussion and reflection, I developed several design propositions for the next stage of the project.
How might a hand-drawn visualisation help people better understand everyday commuting experiences?
How might multiple commuting journeys be compared within a single visual system?
How might route information and personal experience be represented without making the visualisation overly complex?
These questions helped guide my development decisions for the following weeks.
Independent Study
Reflective Summary
This week helped me move from concept exploration towards a more defined project direction.
The feedback reinforced my decision to continue developing the Comparing Commuting Experiences concept.
I realised that clarity would be one of the most important aspects of the project. While there are many possible layers of information that could be included, the visualisation needs to remain easy to understand.
The discussions also encouraged me to think more carefully about how viewers would read and compare information within the map.
Project Development
Following the feedback, I began refining the structure of the project.
I decided to use the University of Auckland as the common starting point for all commuting routes.
I also started developing a basic map framework that could support multiple journeys within a single visualisation.
At this stage, I focused on route structure, travel time, and comparison between journeys. Keeping the visual system simple will make it easier to add additional information in later stages of development.
Changes Made This Week
• Selected Comparing Commuting Experiences as the final project direction.
• Choose the University of Auckland as the common starting point.
• Simplified the map structure to improve readability.
• Began planning how multiple commuting routes could be compared within one visual system.
